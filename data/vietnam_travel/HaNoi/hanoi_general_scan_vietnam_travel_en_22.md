## **Where to eat in Hanoi during the SEA Games**
Got some time to kill between the SEA Games events and want a bite to eat? Hanoi is a great destination for foodies, as it offers a range of cuisine styles to suit a variety of tastes. Think Asian specialties from neighboring countries, Western classics, and must-try Northern Vietnamese dishes. Here are some suggestions for where to eat out near four of the main event locations.
* * *
### Around the Mỹ Đình National Stadium
Most of the action takes place at the main venue of Vietnam's National Sports Complex. Feeling peckish before or after watching the sports at the stadium? There’s plenty to choose from in the area, which is full of glittering skyscrapers and high-end hotels. There are many Korean restaurants, with others including haute French, Vietnamese, Cantonese (John Anthony), Loatian (Khao Lao), and vegan (Tam Chay Vegan). Consider: 
**_Papa’s Chicken_ **
**TYPE:** Korean-style chicken dishes  
**WHERE:** B004, The Manor, Nam Từ Liêm (next to The Garden Center)  
**DISTANCE:** About 2.5km from the stadium (6-8 minutes by car)
  
_**Baek Jeong BBQ**_
**TYPE:** Korean  
**WHERE:** Khu ĐT mới, N05- TT3, Đỗ Đình Thiện, Mỹ Đình, Nam Từ Liêm  
**DISTANCE:** About 2.7km away (7-8 minutes by car)
_**Noori Restaurant**_
**TYPE** : Singaporean  
**WHERE:** 11B CT5 My Dinh Urban Area, Me Tri, Nam Tu Liem District  
**DISTANCE:** About 3km (8 minutes by car)
_**Al Fresco’s The Garden**_
**TYPE:** Italian-style pizza, pasta, steak, salads  
**WHERE:** The Manor, Me Tri Street, My Dinh DO-04 Ground Floor, The Garden Center  
**DISTANCE:** Roughly 3km away (8-10 minutes by car)
**_Akira Back_ **
**TYPE:** Contemporary Japanese  
**WHERE:** JW Marriot Hotel, 8 Đỗ Đức Dục, Mễ Trì, Nam Từ Liêm  
**DISTANCE:** About 4,5km (9 minutes by car)
_**French Grill**_
**TYPE:** Haute French  
**WHERE:** JW Marriot Hotel, 8 Đỗ Đức Dục, Mễ Trì, Nam Từ Liêm  
**DISTANCE:** About 4,5km (9 minutes by car)
_**Keangnam Hanoi Landmark Tower**_
**TYPE:** Various, including Biwon and Sopoong Restaurant, Stellar Steakhouse, 3 Spoons Restaurant, and the high-end The Hive Lounge.  
**WHERE:** Khu E6, Phạm Hùng, Cầu Giấy  
**DISTANCE:** About 4,3km (9 minutes by car)
_**Indochina Plaza**_
**TYPES:** Various restaurants include: Vietstreet, Sushibar 3, King BBQ (Korean), Thai Express IPH, Nan N Kabab Restaurant, Kichi Kichi. For pizza or Italian food, there’s Pizza Hut, Pizza 4Ps and the Pane e Vino Italian Restaurant.  
**WHERE:** 241 Xuân Thủy, Dịch Vọng Hậu, Cầu Giấy  
**DISTANCE:** It’s 4.5 km from My Dinh Stadium (10-12 minutes by car)
_**Paula’s Wines Restaurant (5,8km)**_
**TYPE:** International / Western  
**WHERE:** B1 Mandarin Garden (behind Grand Plaza hotel), P. Hoàng Ngân, Trung Hoà, Cầu Giấy  
**DISTANCE:** About 6km away (12-15 minutes by car)

### Around the Hoàng Mai Stadium
There’s plenty to explore in the stadium surroundings of Hoang Mai, a quieter residential area southeast of the city. A fair selection of restaurants and cafes cater to the many locals and expats living here. Some only require a stroll to the lake. You may have to take a short drive for further options.

_**Fenghuang**_

**WHERE:** XR7H+VFG, Lot CX14 (Next to Linh Dam swimming pool), Khu đô thị Linh Đàm, Hoàng Mai  
**DISTANCE:** About 600m (2 minutes by car)
_**Kombo**_

**WHERE:** Kiot 18 Tòa Nhà Rainbow Linh Đàm, P, Hoàng Mai

### Around the Quan Ngua Sports Complex (Ba Đình District)
Quần Ngựa Sports Palace is in one of the four original districts of Hanoi, in the political heart of the country. Many museums, embassies, and landmarks are found here. Also, many places to eat. 
For Vietnamese classics, you can try places serving the best-quality phở, bánh xèo pancakes, or a steaming bowl of bánh đúc. There’s also bún ngan, a noodle soup made with bamboo shoots and duck meat; chả cá, a legendary Hanoian fried dish; and cà phê trứng, the to-die-for Vietnamese egg coffee. One of the most top-rated bún chả joints in town (Bun Cha Huong Lien) is also found about 6km away.
_**Old Hanoi Restaurant by Master Chef Quan Nguyen**_
**TYPE:** Vietnamese food  
**WHERE:** 18 Ton That Thiep, Ba Dinh district, Ha Noi  
**DISTANCE:** 3 km (a 10-minute by car)
_**Ga Tan Co Bien**_
**TYPE:** Vietnamese chicken and noodle dishes  
**WHERE:** Lane 285 Đội Cấn, Đội Cấn, Ba Đình  
**DISTANCE:** 750m (a 10-minute walk)
_**Cha Ca Ngon Ha Noi**_
**TYPE:** Vietnamese fish dishes  
**WHERE:** 269 P. Kim Mã, Giảng Võ, Ba Đình  
**DISTANCE:** About 2km (6 minutes by car)
_**Hanh Bun Mien Ngan**_
**TYPE:** Vietnamese  
**WHERE:** 63 Cửa Bắc, Quán Thánh, Ba Đình  
**DISTANCE** : 3.7km (10 minutes by car)
_**Banh Duc Nong**_
**TYPE:** Vietnamese (bánh đúc)  
**WHERE:** 45 Châu Long, Quán Thánh, Ba Đình  
**DISTANCE:** 3.7km (10 minutes by car)
_**Giang Cafe**_
**TYPE:** Family-run Vietnamese coffee shop  
**WHERE:** 106 Yên Phụ, Nguyễn Trung Trực, Ba Đình  
**DISTANCE:** 4.6km (13 minutes by car)
