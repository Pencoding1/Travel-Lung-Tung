  * # Blackpink set to rock in Hanoi this July




## **The famous Kpop girl group is finally bringing their concert Born pink at Hanoi's My Dinh National Stadium on 29-30 July. This will be the first time that Blackpink performs in Vietnam.**
* * *
There was no point being a fan these days if you weren’t willing to go the extra mile for your idols. And Vietnam is more than ready to welcome all the Kpop fans around the world to come, rock with us in the Born Pink concert in Hanoi and take your time to explore our beautiful country.
With the capacity of 40,192 seats, with 4 stands, My Dinh Stadium of Ha Noi have been the venue of major international entertainment events, namely:
MTV Exit 2010 with appearance of Super Junior, Kate Miller, Gravity Tour 2011 by Westlife, MBC’s Music Core in 2012, KBS’s Music Bank World Tour in 2015, Asia Artist Awards in 2019, and now BLACKPINK’s Born Pink in July 2023.
While in Ha Noi, Kpop fans can enjoy its scrumptious street food, then stroll down the Old Quarter, jump in a cyclo and tour this intoxicating maze. Modern cafes, world-class restaurants, and cool art galleries are also here to serve you during the concert. Coming to Ha Noi, visitors will have an opportunity to admire the six heritage sites that capture the story and spirit of the city: Thang Long Imperial Citadel; The Temple of Literature; The Old Quarter; Bach Ma and Voi Phuc Temple – two of the four guarding temples of Hanoi; Bach Ma temple: Protector of the east; Voi Phuc temple: Protector of the west. Noi Bai international airport is only 28 km from the city centre. There are many options for getting around Hanoi, including taxis, buses, and motorbikes. Or give it a try, hop-on hop-off bus is a great way to discover Hanoi. There is also the world’s famous railway Reunification Express to travel to major destinations including Sapa (Lao Cai) and Son Doong Cave (Phong Nha Ke Bang, Dong Hoi, Quang Binh).
Save the date, book the service and pack your suitcase. The Born Pink concert in Hanoi is right at your door.
We will see you really soon!
