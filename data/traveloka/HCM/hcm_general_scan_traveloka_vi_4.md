## Về Private Saigon Vegan Foodie Adventure Half Day Tour
When one thinks of Vietnamese food, the usual shirmp spring rolls or beef noodle soup might come to mind. Luckily for vegans and vegetarians, Vietnamese cuisine also has an array of no-meat dishes and diet friendly meals for them to enjoy! Join a half day vegan food tour around Saigon and discover the flavorful and complex dishes without having to worry about the ingredients that were put into the food. Visit markets and food stalls as you ride through the streets of Ho Chi Minh aboard a scooter just like the locals. Refresh yourself with fresh sugarcane juice and try vegan versions of pho noodle soup, spring rolls, Vietnamese pancakes, and more as you go. More than just food, you'll also have the opportunity to learn about Vietnam's culture and history when you make quick stops at the Ngoc Hoang temple, or The Venerated Thich Quang Duc Monument depending on your chosen package. This half day tour will surely satiate your hunger for food and thirst for knowledge all in one go!  

**Enjoy local dessert (passion fruit, blueberry, kiwi,...), coffee flan, che (mixture of beans)**
**Pickup by scooter**
**Taste a savory vegan pancake made from turmeric flour**
**Enjoy authentic coffee and see how people make coffee in the traditional method**
**Visit the oldest condominium in town**
